Quotes for a lawyer's website in Portugal can range from €300 to €6,000. The difference depends on the number of pages, content writing, work to improve visibility on Google, and the maintenance included. Professional duties add editorial decisions for the firm.

I built the website for Dr Bárbara Barbizani and worked through those decisions on a real project. This article helps firms interpret published market prices and prepare a useful request for a quote; it is not legal advice, and the lawyer should approve the final text before publication.

## What Portuguese firms actually pay

- **A basic one-to-three-page site** — home, practice areas, contact — runs roughly €300–€450. [Fassst](https://fassst.pt/setores/advogados/) prices its one-page website and identity package for lawyers at €449, and [SPOT Digital](https://spotdigital.pt/sites-para-advogados/) starts its three-page lawyer package at €299 plus VAT.
- **A full institutional site** — practice areas, team bios, a blog, typically five to fifteen pages — lands between €800 and €4,000, matching [DigitalXperience's market breakdown](https://digitalxperience.pt/custo-criar-um-site-profissional-em-portugal/) for institutional sites generally.
- **Projects intended to appear on Google for highly competitive searches**, such as family law in Lisbon, start near €5,000 and can reach €12,000, according to [VisualMind's 2026 pricing guide](https://visualmind.pt/blog/quanto-custa-website-profissional-portugal-2026).

For many solo lawyers and small firms, the middle bracket provides a suitable scope. It allows the firm to explain its main practice areas, introduce the team, and publish useful information. Firm size alone does not determine the page count.

Writing can cost as much as the visual format. A professional biography requires careful selection and confirmation of facts, while each practice-area page needs clear, accurate wording that respects the profession's duties. If an agency receives scattered notes and has to structure everything, that work should appear in the proposal.

## Costs after publication

Annual costs continue after the build. Domain registration, hosting, and maintenance together typically run €100–€400 a month. The amount varies with updates, security monitoring, backups, technical support, and the frequency of content changes.

Ask suppliers to itemise these costs. A monthly fee may cover hosting alone, or it may also include corrections, small changes, and incident response. Confirm who checks the contact form, how often backups run, and what restoring the site would cost if something goes wrong.

## Advertising and professional duties

Law No. 6/2024 repealed the general advertising prohibition in Article 94 of the Statute of the Portuguese Bar Association. According to the [opinion published by the Ordem dos Advogados](https://portal.oa.pt/comunicacao/imprensa/2024/12/27/advogados-ja-podem-fazer-publicidade-da-atividade-profissional-mas-com-restricoes/), lawyers may advertise their services on websites, social media, and paid adverts while remaining subject to their other professional duties.

The opinion considers invitations to make contact, including subliminal, implicit, or indirect invitations, potential illicit client solicitation. It also recalls the need to protect professional secrecy, avoid conflicts of interest, and verify a client's identity when carrying out legal work online. These points affect both the content and the way a website receives enquiries.

Testimonials and descriptions of cases require particularly careful review. [An analysis of the current rules](https://cristianopinheiro.pt/blog/publicidade-feita-por-advogados-revogacao-artigo-94o-do-estatuto-da-ordem-dos-advogados) discusses limits arising from secrecy and professional duties. Before publishing, the lawyer should confirm that the information may be disclosed, cannot identify a client, and does not suggest a predictable outcome.

A firm can provide useful information in other ways. Its website might explain documents commonly required, the general stages of a matter, or how the firm works. Contact details can remain visible, but the lawyer should approve the wording around them instead of copying sales prompts from an unregulated business.

## What to ask before you sign

Start with the scope: page count, languages, and integrations. A one-page site cannot be compared directly with one covering several practice areas and a full team. The proposal should also state who interviews the lawyers, writes the text, and handles revisions; if the firm supplies the content, reserve internal time to prepare and approve it.

Next, ask what work will help the site appear on Google. Page organisation, titles, descriptions, and ongoing support are separate tasks, so stating that SEO is included does not define the service. Ask the supplier to flag testimonials or case references that need review while keeping final approval within the firm.

Compare hosting, maintenance, support, and future changes over the same period, preferably one year. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a lawyer's website cost in Portugal?

A basic website costs about €300–€450. An institutional website with practice areas, biographies, and a blog typically costs €800–€4,000. Projects aimed at highly competitive Google searches start near €5,000 and can reach €12,000.

### Can lawyers advertise services online?

Law No. 6/2024 repealed the general advertising prohibition. The OA opinion permits advertising on websites, social media, and paid adverts while maintaining professional duties; it also considers direct or indirect invitations to make contact potential illicit client solicitation.

### What annual costs should a firm expect?

Domain registration, hosting, and maintenance together typically run €100–€400 a month, excluding content changes or ongoing work to appear on Google. Ask for a written list of services covered by the monthly fee.

### Is a basic website builder enough?

It can cover a simple online presence. The firm will still need to prepare its content, ensure the site works well on a phone, configure the domain and hosting, and approve the text in light of its professional duties.
