# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitor is a prospective client or business owner evaluating whether to hire for a website, app, or wider digital presence. They arrive from a referral, search, or social profile, often on mobile first, and browse in English or European Portuguese. Their job to be done is to quickly judge whether this person is credible and skilled, understand what services and price ranges are on offer, and take the next step without friction.

A secondary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating André Rodrigo for senior software engineering roles. They read the same portfolio and experience as proof of seniority and big-tech pedigree rather than as a service menu, so the work must satisfy both a "should I hire this person for my project" and a "should I hire this person for my team" read.

## Product Purpose

This is the portfolio and services site for André Rodrigo, a Senior Software Engineer offering website and app development plus AI and automation consulting. It exists to turn interested visitors into booked consultations by proving credibility through real client work, measurable results, and testimonials, and by making the services and pricing legible enough that the next step feels obvious. Success is a booked consultation call and qualified inbound interest, whether from a client or a recruiter.

## Positioning

Fast, secure, custom-built websites and digital solutions that drive real business results, delivered by a senior engineer rather than an agency or a template.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: request a quote via the dedicated budget Typeform popup ("Request a quote" / "Pedir orçamento"), highlighted across the homepage, pricing, and contact sections. Secondary CTA: book a free consultation call via a second Typeform popup ("Schedule a call"). For visitors not yet ready to talk: browse the portfolio and case studies.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a senior engineer who builds fast, secure, custom sites that get real results.
- Belief ladder: first, this is a credible senior engineer with recognizable clients and companies behind him; then, he builds bespoke, high-performance work rather than templates; then, that work translates into real business outcomes such as a reported 300% increase in customer interactions; then, the process is clear and low-risk, with defined stages, transparent pricing, and a free consultation; therefore booking a call is the natural next step.
- Proof on hand: peer and client testimonials in `constants/testimonials.constants.ts`; a portfolio of client and company projects (CDEFF, Bárbara Barbizani, Filipa Marques, Carolina Jardim, Paixão Med, and others) in `data/info.data.tsx`; and trusted-company logos spanning Snyk, Navro, Pipedrive, Peacock / NBCUniversal, and Probely.

## Brand Personality

The voice is professional, direct, and technically credible, confident without tipping into cold corporate tone. Copy leans on clear calls to action and benefit-oriented language about speed, performance, and outcomes. In three words the brand is modern, credible, and approachable, and the feeling a visitor should walk away with is trust and confidence in the work paired with the sense that this is a real, approachable person to talk to. The modern, glass-forward aesthetic is itself part of the pitch: it signals current, high-craft engineering and reinforces that the site is the product. The visual execution of that aesthetic lives in DESIGN.md.

## Anti-references

This should not look like a generic AI landing page, with body text set in Inter on a white background and clichéd purple gradients. It should also avoid the cheap template or site-builder aesthetic of a generic Wix or Squarespace build; the site is itself the strongest argument for hiring a custom developer, so it must never look like something a builder could produce.

## Design Principles

Show, don't tell: prove credibility with real client work, named companies, measurable outcomes, and testimonials rather than adjectives. Practice what you preach: the site is a live portfolio piece, so it must be fast, polished, and bespoke to the standard being sold. Guide to the call: every page offers a clear next step toward a consultation, with the portfolio as the low-commitment fallback. Locale parity: English and European Portuguese are both first-class, with the same layout, quality, and motion in each. Approachable authority: deliver senior, big-tech credibility in a warm, human, direct voice rather than a faceless agency one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG level is required. Aim for sensible contrast on the dark-first theme, where white primary text and a magenta-pink accent sit on a deep background, and keep body copy readable rather than washed out. Ensure interactive elements have visible focus states, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` given the site's heavy use of Framer Motion so that motion-sensitive visitors get a calm experience.
